First, make sure you have a Tinkerer's Workshop

You need it for the GPS, R.E.K. 3000, Goblin Tech, Fish Finder, PDA, and Cell Phone.

Defeat a Goblin Army, then explore the Cavern layer for the Bound Goblin. Talk to him to free him and provide a vacant house. Once he moves in as the Goblin Tinkerer, buy the Tinkerer's Workshop from him for 10 Gold.

Craft the GPS

Tracks time, depth, and horizontal position.

Crafting station: Tinkerer's Workshop

Choose either watch. Mine Gold or Platinum Ore and smelt it into bars at a Furnace. At a Work Bench, craft the watch from 10 matching bars + 1 Chain. For the Chain: craft it from 1 Iron Bar or Lead Bar at an Iron Anvil or Lead Anvil. Each bar is made by smelting 3 matching ore at a Furnace.

Wiki · Wiki

Farm these enemies for a chance to drop it: Crawdad, Giant Shelly, Salamander, Piranha, Mother Slime, Snow Flinx, Undead Viking, or Armored Viking. These enemies appear in their corresponding underground, jungle, water, or snow areas.

Wiki

Farm Cave Bats, Jungle Bats, Ice Bats, Giant Bats, Spore Bats, Crawdads, Giant Shellys, or Salamanders. Bats have a 1% drop chance; Crawdads, Giant Shellys, and Salamanders have a 1.25% chance.

Craft the R.E.K. 3000

Combines enemy count, nearby creature detection, and radar information.

Crafting station: Tinkerer's Workshop

Search Surface Chests (about 1 in 11). It can also come from Wooden or Pearlwood Crates while fishing (about 1 in 120), or be sold by the Skeleton Merchant during a Waxing Gibbous for 2 Gold 50 Silver.

Wiki

After defeating Skeletron, farm Dungeon enemies: Angry Bones, Cursed Skulls, Librarian Skeletons, and Dark Casters. Each has a 1% chance to drop it.

Wiki

Buy it from the Traveling Merchant for 5 Gold when it appears. Check his inventory each visit.

Combines valuable ore detection, movement speed, and damage-per-second information.

Crafting station: Tinkerer's Workshop

Defeat the Nymph, also known as the Lost Girl, in the Cavern layer. It has a 50% drop chance in Classic mode and a 100% chance in Expert/Master modes.

Craft the PDA

Combine the four accessories made in steps 1–4.

Crafting station: Tinkerer's Workshop

Craft it at a Tinkerer's Workshop using 1 Gold Watch or Platinum Watch, 1 Compass, and 1 Depth Meter. The watch can use either metal type; the other two items are enemy drops.

Wiki

Craft it at a Tinkerer's Workshop using 1 Radar, 1 Tally Counter, and 1 Lifeform Analyzer.

Wiki

Craft it at a Tinkerer's Workshop using 1 Metal Detector, 1 Stopwatch, and 1 DPS Meter.

Wiki

Craft it at a Tinkerer's Workshop using 1 Fisherman's Pocket Guide, 1 Weather Radio, and 1 Sextant. All three are random Angler quest rewards.

Wiki
6

Get a mirror

You only need one of these two mirror options.

Crafting station: Tinkerer's Workshop

Find one in Gold Chests in the Underground or Cavern layers. You can also craft one at a Furnace: 10 Glass + 8 Gold Bars or 8 Platinum Bars + 3 Diamonds. Make Glass from 2 Sand at a Furnace (so 20 Sand for 10 Glass).

Wiki · Wiki

Search Frozen Chests in the Ice biome. It works as the Magic Mirror alternative for the Cell Phone recipe.

Wiki

7. Craft the Cell Phone!

At the Tinkerer's Workshop, combine:

1 PDA + 1 Magic Mirror OR 1 Ice Mirror

That's it — you've crafted the Cell Phone! It provides useful information and lets you teleport back to your spawn point.
