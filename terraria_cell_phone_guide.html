Terraria crafting walkthrough

📱 Craft the Cell Phone

Follow the steps, collect each ingredient, and tick off the checklist as you go. You’ll combine the four information accessories into a PDA, then turn it into a Cell Phone.

Tinkerer's Workshop required
Your crafting progress0 / 15 checked
1

Craft the GPS

The GPS tracks time, depth, and location information.
Combine at a Tinkerer's Workshop

Watch crafting: 10 Gold or Platinum Bars + 1 Chain, crafted at a Table and Chair. Compass and Depth Meter are dropped by certain enemies.

2

Craft the R.E.K. 3000

Combines radar, enemy-counting, and nearby-life detection.
Combine at a Tinkerer's Workshop

Radar: found in surface and underground chests. Tally Counter: dropped by enemies in the Dungeon. Lifeform Analyzer: sold by the Traveling Merchant.

3

Craft the Goblin Tech

Shows nearby metal, movement speed, and damage per second.
Combine at a Tinkerer's Workshop

Metal Detector: dropped by Nymphs (Lost Girls). Stopwatch and DPS Meter: sold by the Traveling Merchant.

4

Craft the Fish Finder

Combines the Angler's fishing information accessories.
Combine at a Tinkerer's Workshop

All three are random rewards for completing Angler fishing quests. Keep doing quests if you haven't received them yet.

5

Craft the PDA

You need all four accessories made in steps 1–4.
Combine at a Tinkerer's Workshop
6

Find a Magic Mirror

This is the teleportation part of the recipe.

Find a Magic Mirror in Underground or Cavern chests, or an Ice Mirror in Ice biome chests. You only need one.

🎉

7. Craft the Cell Phone!

At the Tinkerer's Workshop, combine:

1 PDA + 1 Magic Mirror or Ice Mirror

That's it — you've crafted the Cell Phone! It provides lots of useful information and lets you teleport home.
